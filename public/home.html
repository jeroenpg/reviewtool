reviewtool

Reviews

Rounds of screenshots to judge. Agents publish rounds and answer your comments; you decide what stays.

    How a round goes 1. An agent publishes screenshots with reviewtool round <name> manifest.json; each page shows whether it changed since the last round. 2. You compare, pin comments on the spots that matter, give each page a verdict, and press Send to agent. 3. The agent, waiting with reviewtool wait, picks it up, fixes it, replies, and publishes the next round. 4. reviewtool log --post keeps the whole history on the pull request.
